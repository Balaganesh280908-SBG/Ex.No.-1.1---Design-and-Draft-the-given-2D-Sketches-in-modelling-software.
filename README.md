# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.

## REQUIREMENTS

  1. Autodesk fusion 360
  2. Windows 10
  3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
  4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
  
## PROCEDURE

## Step 1 
Open Autodesk Fusion 360 and create a new design file.Select the Create Sketch option and choose the XY plane.



Draw a vertical line extending 65 mm above the centre point and another vertical line extending 50 mm below the centre point.



Draw two concentric circles of diameter 66 mm and 40 mm at the centre point.Select the origin point carefully before starting the sketch.




Use the Sketch Dimension tool to enter the required measurements accurately.Check the alignment of the vertical lines and circles.

<img width="290" height="493" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/241d0a32-3309-47ca-80ad-0dda402ba358" />

## Step 2
At the 65 mm point from the centre, draw two concentric circles of diameter 20 mm and 32 mm.



At the 50 mm point from the centre, draw two concentric circles of diameter 10 mm and 20 mm.



Draw two more circles of diameter 20 mm and 14 mm on the left and right centre points.These centres are located 47 mm to the left and right of the vertical centre line and 26 mm below the origin.



Use the Sketch Dimension tool to maintain the specified measurements.Check the position and alignment of all the circles according to the given sketch.

## Step 3 

Draw two circles of diameter 128 mm and 40 mm.Apply suitable Tangent constraints with the other circles to obtain a smooth profile.



Draw another two circles of diameter 40 mm and 50 mm.Apply suitable Tangent constraints with the neighbouring circles to complete the curve.



Select the required circles carefully while applying the Tangent constraint.Make sure the curves are connected smoothly without unwanted gaps.



Check whether the outer profile matches the given reference sketch.

<img width="306" height="425" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a22fed38-14ec-4325-a116-2445531f41f4" />

## Step 4 
Draw a 12 mm diameter circle on the 66 mm circle.Select the Circular Pattern command from the Sketch Create menu.



Choose the 12 mm circle as the object and select the centre of the main sketch as the pattern centre.



Create 6 copies of the circle using the Circular Pattern command.Check whether all six circles are equally distributed.



Use the Trim command to remove the excess portions of the sketch.Remove unwanted lines carefully without affecting the required profile.



Check the final sketch to ensure that the shape is clear and properly formed

<img width="262" height="410" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/54862da1-f979-40d8-aa59-e72cde92ddd4" />

## Step 5 
Select the portions of the sketch to be mirrored.Choose the vertical centre line as the Mirror Line and apply the Mirror command.



Check whether both sides of the sketch are properly aligned and symmetrical.Make sure all the required portions are selected correctly.



Once the sketch is complete, click Finish Sketch.Select the completed profile and use the Extrude command.



Extrude the profile by a distance of -20 mm.Check the final 3D model to ensure that the required shape and thickness are obtained.

## Step 6

After creating the 3D model, go to Design and choose Drawing.Select the From Design option to create a new drawing.Choose the required drawing settings, such as ISO standard, millimetre units and A3 sheet size.



Place the front view, top view, side view and isometric view on the drawing sheet.Arrange all four views properly with suitable spacing.



Use the Dimension tool to add the required measurements.Check that all dimensions are clearly visible and do not overlap.



Finally, review the drawing and save the completed design.
